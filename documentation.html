  • Top breathing zone: x 7–93%, y 5–16%
  • Key visual safe zone: x 42–92%, y 10–78%

Mobile / centered content

Mobile

  • Ratio: 9:16
  • Crop-risk perimeter: 6% left/right, 8% top, 14% bottom
  • Top breathing zone: x 8–92%, y 5–15%
  • Key visual safe zone: x 18–82%, y 10–56%
  • Copy-safe zone: x 8–92%, y 64–90%
  • CTA + helper zone: x 12–88%, y 76–88%

Crop first: Mobile keeps 9:16 in normal cases, but on shorter heights the lower part of the artwork is what gets cropped first.

Place the key visual: Keep the subject in the upper or upper-middle part of the frame and avoid bright or high-detail elements behind the lower content stack.

Runtime crop behavior

  • All hero media uses object-fit: cover.
  • On taller viewports the authored focus position is used as-is.
  • When the hero becomes height-constrained, horizontal focus is preserved but vertical crop becomes top-anchored.
  • In practice, the lower part of the artwork is what gets cropped first.

What This Means for Art Direction

  • Keep critical subject matter in the upper or upper-middle part of the frame.
  • Keep lower bands more tolerant of copy overlap and bottom-first cropping.
  • Desktop remains an inset card, while tablet and mobile carry the header over the creative from first paint.
  • Tablet and mobile may require extra scroll on short or rotated screens rather than shrinking the hero too much.

Product

Use the modules deliberately so the hero tells one clear story at a time.

The live demo exposes multiple switches, but in product usage each one should answer a specific communication need rather than simply adding more surface area to the banner.

Copy limits

  • Larger title: up to about `32` characters including spaces.
  • Large title: up to about `48` characters including spaces.
  • Subtitle: about `70` characters, assuming the creative tolerates up to 3 lines on desktop.
  • Label: about `24` characters.
  • Helper text: about `72` characters.

Title

Use when
Keep the title as the main message of the hero and use the larger size only for short, punchy headlines.
Avoid when
The title becomes too long, wraps awkwardly, or starts fighting the artwork for attention.
Impact
The title defines the hierarchy of the entire content stack and directly affects how much room is left for the subtitle below it.

Subtitle

Use when
Add one supporting layer of context that helps the title land without overloading it.
Avoid when
The subtitle repeats the title, becomes body copy, or pushes the stack too deep for the creative.
Impact
The safe area supports up to 3 lines on desktop, but shorter copy keeps the banner cleaner across all breakpoints.

Logo

Purpose
Add event or property branding above the label.
Use when
The logo adds recognition without crowding the art.
Avoid when
The artwork already contains logos, badges, or small detailed marks.
Impact
Adds brand signal but increases visual density in the stack.

Label

Purpose
Surface the event date range, promo note, or discount message.
Use when
A short timestamp or offer cue improves scanning.
Avoid when
The title already carries the same information.
Impact
Adds a small pre-title module without changing the full hierarchy.

Helper text

Purpose
Handle conditions, rights notes, or legal clarification.
Use when
Extra context is required but should stay secondary.
Avoid when
The text is non-essential and only adds visual weight.
Impact
Extends the vertical footprint of the bottom content stack.
