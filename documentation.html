DAZN Hero System

React card hero with image or video media, measured glow, and breakpoint-driven art direction

The live hero is now a Vite + React implementation with one desktop card, one square tablet card, and one portrait mobile card. The glow is sampled from the active still or live video frame at runtime and stays centered inside the hero wrapper while the card always follows the parent width.

Runtime

React + Vite

Componentized header and hero, measured with ResizeObserver, built with a multi-page Vite setup.

Breakpoint Cards

Each breakpoint uses a different card behavior

Desktop 16:9 overlay card video-example-desktop.mov

Desktop

  • Applies at 1025px and above
  • Hero stays visually close to the current cinematic desktop version
  • Card keeps the wide overlay composition and bottom-left content stack
  • Width always follows the parent and the height cap is calculated from the available viewport after the header
  • Minimum outer gutter is 64px
  • Card radius is 18px
Tablet Top-aligned square art video-example-tablet.mov

Tablet

  • Applies from 768px to 1024px
  • 1:1 is the creative target ratio, not a hard live lock
  • Width always follows the parent and height caps at 85% of the available viewport after the header
  • Once capped, the card crops vertically instead of freezing horizontal resize
  • Artwork keeps a slightly upward crop bias so a small bottom strip stays visible
  • Card radius is 14px with 32px outer gutter
Mobile Top-aligned 5:7 art video-example-mobile.mov

Mobile

  • Applies at 767px and below
  • Hero remains inset, never full-bleed
  • 5:7 is the creative target ratio, not a hard live lock
  • Width always follows the parent and height caps at 90% of the available viewport after the header
  • Once capped, the card crops vertically instead of freezing horizontal resize
  • Artwork keeps a slightly upward crop bias so a small bottom strip stays visible
  • Card radius is 14px with 16px outer gutter

Glow + Border

The glow is sampled at runtime and the hero uses a two-layer border

Glow Engine

  • Glow exists inside .hero-banner-demo only
  • The glow layer is centered on the live hero card, not the viewport
  • Geometry is updated with ResizeObserver
  • Glow geometry is clamped so it never widens the layout or causes body overflow
  • Stills are sampled once; videos are sampled from live frames at a throttled rate
  • Artwork colors are sampled from top, left, right, and lower-mid regions
  • Poster or fallback colors are used before sampling completes or if sampling fails

Border Stack

  • Base border: 1px solid white at 10% opacity
  • Overlay border uses a masked vertical gradient
  • 0%: white at 0% opacity
  • 30%: #FFFA00 at 60%
  • 50%: #FFAA00 at 40%
  • 70%: #64FCDC at 20%
  • 100%: white at 0% opacity

Creative Guidance

Keep the focus area high and leave the lower copy zone calm

The live copy always occupies the lower portion of the card. The media should carry the story higher in the frame so the copy and CTA can remain readable without flattening the art. Motion uses the same safe-space and crop rules as stills.

Desktop Creative Rules

  • Keep the main subject on the right half of the 16:9 frame
  • Leave the lower-left text zone darker and quieter
  • Avoid bright faces or highlights behind the left copy block
  • Desktop still uses one landscape master across large widths

Tablet and Mobile Rules

  • Keep the subject in the upper-middle of the square and mobile art
  • The lower third of the card should stay calmer for live copy
  • Tablet art should be composed to survive a square target ratio with vertical crop
  • Mobile art must still survive the tighter 5:7 live frame and height cap
